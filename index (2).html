Benjamin Atawiah

PhD Candidate in Mathematics  ·  The University of Alabama

I am a PhD candidate in Mathematics at the University of Alabama, advised by Dr. Zheng Sun. My research focuses on numerical methods for partial differential equations, with emphasis on discontinuous Galerkin finite element methods and high-order time integration.

I study stability and error analysis of Runge–Kutta DG methods, Fourier analysis of filtered DG schemes, and efficient time-stepping algorithms for high-order PDE discretizations.

Research

My work lies at the intersection of numerical analysis and scientific computing, with a focus on high-order discretization and time integration for time-dependent PDEs. I am particularly interested in rigorous mathematical analysis — stability, accuracy, and convergence — of DG-based schemes.

  • Stability analysis of Runge–Kutta discontinuous Galerkin methods
  • Energy-based error estimates for high-order DG schemes
  • Fourier analysis of filtered DG methods
  • Local time stepping and CFL optimization for DG discretizations
  • Exponential time differencing methods for dispersive equations
  • Local discontinuous Galerkin methods with compact stencils
